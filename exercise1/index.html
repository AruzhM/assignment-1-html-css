Hedy Lamarr

About Hedy Lamarr

Hedy Lamarr was born on September 9, 1914, in Vienna, Austria. She was an actress and inventor who became known for her work in wireless communication. During World War II, she and George Antheil developed a frequency-hopping system for secure radio communication. Hedy Lamarr died on January 19, 2000, in Florida, USA.

Important Events in Hedy Lamarr's Life

  1. 1914 — Hedy Lamarr was born in Vienna, Austria.
  2. 1930s — She became a successful actress in European and American cinema.
  3. 1940 — She and George Antheil developed a frequency-hopping communication system.
  4. 1942 — Their invention was patented in the United States.
  5. 1997 — Lamarr received recognition for her contribution to technology.
  6. 2000 — Hedy Lamarr died in Florida, USA.

Her Inventions and Contributions

  • Frequency-hopping technology for secure radio communication
  • Wireless communication research and invention
  • Contribution to ideas that influenced modern wireless technologies
